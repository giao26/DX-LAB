- (direction) Create mode, express distillation from final PRD, final UX and final Architecture Spine; slug dx-lab.
- (decision) Why: deliver an open-source OLP 2026 demo where one traceable ticket crosses H people and access, P process, D operational data and I human-approved AI improvement, while remaining reproducible and contribution-friendly.
- (capability) CAP-1 Portal navigation: a user can enter H and open the P process overview, approved Resources and authorized work surfaces; success when every route opens the intended function and drafts never appear as published knowledge.
- (capability) CAP-2 Approved knowledge: staff can find and identify the active version and approval state of SOP and FAQ content; success when only published versions are shown and indexed for official AI grounding.
- (capability) CAP-3 Authorized internal notification: the assigned employee receives one Odoo notification linked to the ticket; success when group and resource authorization is rechecked, duplicates are suppressed and failed delivery is retryable and visible.
- (capability) CAP-4 Valid ticket intake: a customer can submit required contact data, request type, description and one image or PDF up to 10 MB; success when invalid data creates nothing, valid intake creates a system code and timestamp, reconciles the customer record safely and records confirmation-email delivery.
- (capability) CAP-5 Human-confirmed AI classification: AI proposes Complaint, Consultation or Warranty from description text only and the assigned employee confirms or corrects it before processing; success when all three type values and confirmation audit are stored, fallback works without AI and cross-group correction reallocates safely.
- (capability) CAP-6 Fair assignment: P distributes work among available employees in the mapped group by lowest accepted-assignment count and ascending-ID rotation; success when one-active-ticket capacity, atomic reservation, FIFO waiting and concurrency tests preserve fairness.
- (capability) CAP-7 Lifecycle and SLA: the assignee records process steps and moves a ticket from Waiting to In progress to Closed under a two-business-hour SLA; success when close requires a result, closed work cannot silently reopen, every wait counts under the configured calendar and corrections remain auditable.
- (capability) CAP-8 Closure and CSAT: closure sends one result and survey invitation, then accepts one secure 1-to-5-star response; success when no-response differs from low score, 1-to-2 stars create one team-lead review and delivery retry never duplicates the semantic message.
- (capability) CAP-9 Scoped access: each actor can view ticket and metric data only for their role, group and assignment; success when API, Odoo, exports, attachments and embedded analytics deny cross-scope access and revocation takes effect.
- (capability) CAP-10 Operational dashboard and daily snapshot: authorized users can inspect new, backlog, closed, SLA, step-time and CSAT metrics and drill to evidence; success when committed changes appear within 60 seconds and each idempotent daily snapshot records scope, period, metric version and source trace.
- (capability) CAP-11 Evidence-based bottleneck detection: P detects at least three overdue warranty tickets received within seven calendar days sharing an inspection step longer than 60 business minutes and requests an AI explanation; success when two tickets or exactly 60 minutes do not trigger, evidence is masked and the same evidence creates one recommendation.
- (capability) CAP-12 Human-governed SOP improvement: the director accepts or rejects a recommendation and acceptance creates an AI SOP draft for Odoo knowledge review; success when every decision is audited, drafts stay unpublished and only reviewer approval publishes a version into Resources and AI indexing.
- (capability) CAP-13 First contribution journey: a newcomer can run a lightweight ticket slice, change one rule or notification template, run behavior tests and submit a pull request; success when the path needs no AI model and a relevant regression makes the tests fail.
- (constraint) P is the only writer of canonical ticket, assignment, lifecycle, CSAT, recommendation and SOP state; all other surfaces use versioned contracts or committed events.
- (constraint) Keycloak OIDC subject is the actor identity; authorization is enforced at each resource, delegated Odoo actions preserve user and client identity, and email never serves as an identity key.
- (constraint) AI is advisory, receives minimized data, cannot mutate tickets, publish SOPs or send external decisions, and every impactful result requires the specified human confirmation or approval.
- (constraint) Ticket SLA is two business hours using Monday to Friday 08:00-12:00 and 13:00-17:00 Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh minus configured holidays; assignment, inspection and parts waits count.
- (constraint) The demo is web-online only, Vietnamese-first, usable on desktop and mobile browsers, and custom surfaces target WCAG 2.2 AA at 320 CSS px and 200 percent zoom.
- (constraint) The product is AGPL-3.0, builds from public source with pinned dependencies and licenses, exports business data and approved knowledge in open formats, and offers a core profile that runs without ERP, BI or AI models.
- (constraint) Customer PII and private attachments never enter AI classification; group lists and chat projections remain redacted; attachments are private image or PDF files limited to 10 MB and served only by authorized P endpoints.
- (constraint) Committed updates are replay-safe through idempotency, transactional outbox, durable consumer inbox, aggregate ordering, retry and dead-letter state; audit history is append-only.
- (constraint) Dashboard, daily snapshots and AI evidence share P-owned metric definitions; reporting access uses immutable group scope and deny-by-default row-level security.
- (constraint) Daily backup runs at 00:30 Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh for databases, attachments, Odoo filestore and published knowledge, copies encrypted archives off-host, retains 7 daily 4 weekly 12 monthly copies and records monthly restore evidence.
- (constraint) Development, ephemeral test and reproducible demo environments use separate secrets, databases, volumes and identity clients; Caddy is the only public ingress and privileged services stay private.
- (decision) Non-goals: full ERP or CRM replacement, customer ticket-tracking portal, offline sync, camera or GPS, autonomous AI decisions, automatic SOP publication, model retraining on customer data, enterprise lakehouse or data fabric, predictive ticket forecasting without adequate history, high-availability or multi-region production topology, and live backup restoration during judging.
- (decision) Success signal: the presenter creates one real ticket and follows the same code through H, P, D and I, demonstrates validation, fair assignment, employee confirmation, two-hour SLA, scoped access, dashboard freshness, evidence-based AI recommendation and human-approved SOP flow; a clean-source newcomer also completes the lightweight contribution path.
- (assumption) Historical demo fixtures for the bottleneck scenario are preloaded and visibly labeled as sample data.

- (question) Before packaging the demo, which sample accounts represent customer, employee, team lead, department head, director and knowledge reviewer in the isolated browser sessions?
- (question) When the detailed OLP 2026 prompt is published in November 2026, which scope or release changes does it require?
- (question) Before production use, what legal and operational retention periods apply to tickets, attachments and approved knowledge?
